# Lucent glyphs

Monoline glyphs on a 24 grid, drawn to sit next to Outfit. Generated by `node icons/build.mjs`; edit the script, not the output.

- `svg/*.svg`: one file per glyph at a 1.5 stroke, painting `currentColor`.
- `packages/angular/src/icon/glyphs.ts`: the same glyphs as shapes, rendered by `<lucent-icon>`.

## Style

- Flat ends and round joins, like Outfit's terminals and joins.
- Control glyphs (close, check, chevrons, arrows) are plain lines.
- Circles and rounded containers are open at the top right. The warning triangle stays closed, search is cut where the handle meets the lens, and glyphs made of several parts stay closed. The cut shrinks when a glyph needs the corner (lock).
- Optical sizing: the rendered stroke stays near constant, 1.5px at 24px down to 1.25px at 14px. In the kit this is `--_stroke` with non-scaling strokes; see `styles/components/icon.css`.

## Names

`<lucent-icon name>` takes a Lucent name or a Material Icons name. Material names Lucent draws resolve through the alias table in `build.mjs` (`more_vert` and `more_horiz` both become `more`, `content_copy` becomes `copy`, and so on). Any other name renders from the Material Icons font.
